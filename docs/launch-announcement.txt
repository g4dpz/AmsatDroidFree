Subject: AmsatDroid is now on Google Play

Hi all,

When I first wrote about AmsatDroid here, I promised to follow up with
the Google Play link once it was live. It is now available to everyone:

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid

AmsatDroid is a free, open source amateur satellite pass predictor for
Android 10 or later, on phones and tablets. It has:
  - Pass predictions for your location, with a minimum elevation filter
  - A sky chart with the live position, compass-oriented, and an Aim mode
    that tells you which way to turn and tilt the phone
  - Doppler-corrected uplink and downlink frequencies for each
    transponder mode, from Peter Goodhall's (MM9SQL) OscarWatch database
  - Choose your satellites: the app then offers only the ones you work
  - A Contact planner: when a satellite is above the horizon at both your
    station and another one, with the best moment and the look angles at
    both ends
  - A ground-track map, an overview of what is up now, pass alerts
    (spoken if you like), a next-pass widget and a red night mode

There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it collects no data.

Thank you to everyone who tested it over the last few weeks. Your
reports shaped the setup guide, the satellite chooser and much more.
If you'd like to try new versions before they reach everyone, join the
AmsatDroid group with the Google account you use in the Play Store:
https://groups.google.com/g/amsatdroid
then copy this link into Chrome, signed in with the same account, and
tap "Become a tester":
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid

The user guide and privacy policy are on the project wiki:
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/wiki
The source code (GPL-3.0-or-later) is at
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree

Bugs and feature requests:
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/issues/new/choose
or email me at dave@g4dpz.me.uk (not a reply to the list).

73,
Dave, G4DPZ

Subject: AmsatDroid is live on Google Play - thank you

Hi

AmsatDroid is now live on Google Play, open to everyone:
https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid

Thank you. You installed a test version, tried it against real passes
and told me what didn't work or didn't make sense. Your reports led to
the setup guide, choosing your own satellites, the minimum elevation
filter, Android 10 support and a long list of fixes. It is a much better
app than the one you first installed.

You don't need to do anything. The app on your device keeps working and
keeps getting updates from the Play Store.

You have a choice about what happens next:

- Stay in the test. You'll get each new version a little before
  everyone else, which helps me catch problems before they reach all
  users. The next big change is a new layout for the pass list.

- Or leave the test. Open
  https://play.google.com/apps/testing/uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid
  in Chrome and tap "Leave the program". You keep the app and get the
  public releases instead. Testers can't leave public reviews, so once
  you've left, a review on the Play listing would help other operators
  find it.

Would you like me to remove your email address from the tester list in
Google Play? I keep it only while you're in the test. Just reply
"remove me" and I'll delete it.
Removing it also takes you out of the test, but you keep the app and
get the public releases from the Play Store.

Please keep the reports coming: in the AmsatDroid group
(https://groups.google.com/g/amsatdroid), on the GitHub form
(https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/issues/new/choose), or by
replying to this email.

Thanks again, and 73
Dave, G4DPZ
