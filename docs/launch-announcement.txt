Subject: AmsatDroid is now on Google Play

Hi all,

When I first wrote about AmsatDroid here, I promised to follow up with
the Google Play link once it was live. It is now available to everyone:

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid

AmsatDroid is a free, open source amateur satellite pass predictor for
Android 10 or later, on phones and tablets. It has:
  - Pass predictions for your location, with a minimum elevation filter
  - A sky chart with the live position, compass-oriented, and an Aim mode
    that tells you which way to turn and tilt the phone
  - Doppler-corrected uplink and downlink frequencies for each
    transponder mode, from Peter Goodhall's (MM9SQL) OscarWatch database
  - Choose your satellites: the app then offers only the ones you work
  - A Contact planner: when a satellite is above the horizon at both your
    station and another one, with the best moment and the look angles at
    both ends
  - A ground-track map, an overview of what is up now, pass alerts
    (spoken if you like), a next-pass widget and a red night mode

There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and it collects no data.

Thank you to everyone who tested it over the last few weeks. Your
reports shaped the setup guide, the satellite chooser and much more.
If you'd like to try new versions before they reach everyone, join the
AmsatDroid group:
https://groups.google.com/g/amsatdroid

The user guide, privacy policy and source code (GPL-3.0) are on the
project wiki:
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/wiki

Bugs and feature requests:
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/issues/new/choose
or email me at dave@g4dpz.me.uk (not a reply to the list).

73,
Dave, G4DPZ
