AmsatDroid Free is back! 🛰️

I've resurrected AmsatDroid Free as a brand-new Android app - rebuilt from the
ground up in Kotlin with a modern interface, keeping the proven SGP4/SDP4 orbit
maths underneath.

Track amateur satellite passes with a live sky view and ground-track map,
Doppler-corrected up/downlink frequencies (from Peter Goodhall MM9SQL's
OscarWatch database), pass alerts, a compass sky chart and aiming aid, a next-
pass widget, and a red night mode for observing after dark.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0), for Android 11+. Source and screenshots here:
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree

Coming to the Google Play Store in a few days - I'll post the link when it's
live. 73, Dave G4DPZ
