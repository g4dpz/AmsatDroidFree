Subject: AmsatDroid: more testers wanted for the Google Play closed test

Hi all,

Thanks to everyone who replied to my earlier post about AmsatDroid, my
free, open source amateur satellite pass predictor for Android: passes
with a sky chart and map, Doppler-corrected frequencies from the
OscarWatch database, pass alerts, an aiming aid and a next-pass widget.
It is now in a closed test on Google Play. Google needs at least 12
testers to stay in that test for 14 days before I can apply for
production access. A few more testers would make sure we get there, so
if you have an Android phone or tablet, I'd be grateful for your help.

A lot has been added since that first announcement. Version 2.3.0 now
has:
  - A short setup guide on first start: your location, then a satellite
  - Choose your satellites: the app then offers only the ones you work
  - A Contact planner: when a satellite is above the horizon at both your
    station and another one, with the best moment and the look angles at
    both ends
  - A minimum elevation filter for passes, and a Sky view button for a
    satellite with no passes, such as QO-100
  - An option to centre the world map on your location
    - Support for Android 10 (it needed Android 11 before)

To test you need an Android phone or tablet running Android 10 or later,
and a Google account (Google requires one to join a Play test).

How to join:
  1. Join the AmsatDroid group, using the Google account you use in the
     Play Store on your device:
     https://groups.google.com/g/amsatdroid
  2. Copy this link into Chrome, signed in with the same account, and tap
     "Become a tester":
     https://play.google.com/apps/testing/uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid
  3. Install AmsatDroid from the Play Store.

  >>> USE THE SAME GOOGLE ACCOUNT IN THE GROUP, CHROME AND THE PLAY
  >>> STORE. If the link says "Item not found", a different account is
  >>> almost always signed in somewhere. Don't tap the link inside an
  >>> email or social media app; copy it into Chrome.

Please stay in the test for at least 14 days. A 20-minute guided test
script is on the wiki:
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/wiki/Test-Script
The help and privacy policy are on the wiki too. The app has no ads, no
accounts and no tracking.

Questions and ideas are welcome in the group. Bugs and feature requests
go on the GitHub form (needs a free GitHub account):
https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/issues/new/choose
With any report please give your device, Android version and the app
version (menu, then About). Please don't post your exact home
coordinates; a four-character locator such as IO91 is enough.

If you can't use the group, email me directly (not a reply to the list)
at dave@g4dpz.me.uk.

Thanks, and 73,
Dave, G4DPZ

Hi

Thanks for signing up to test AmsatDroid, the amateur radio satellite pass predictor for Android. It needs Android 10 or later.

The Google account you gave me must be the one in use in two places:

- The Play Store: tap your profile picture at the top right. If it shows another account, tap the account name and switch.
- The browser that opens the link: the test page checks the browser's Google account, not the Play Store's, and a link tapped inside Gmail, Facebook or WhatsApp often opens with a different account. If either is wrong, the link says "Item not found". That means the wrong account somewhere, not that the test is broken.

To install:

1. Paste this link into Chrome on your Android device and tap "Become a tester":

https://play.google.com/apps/testing/uk.me.g4dpz.amsatdroid

2. Tap "Download it on Google Play" on that page and install as normal.

The link can take an hour or two to work after you sign up. If it still says "Item not found" after that, reply with a screenshot, the account shown in the Play Store and in Chrome, and your Android version, and I'll sort it out.

What to try:

Please follow the test script, a guided tour of everything to try. It takes about 20 minutes, plus one real pass:

https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/wiki/Test-Script

If you only have a few minutes: set your home location (menu, Settings), pick a satellite and press Calculate pass, then tap a pass for the sky view. Long-press a pass to set an alert.

Please keep it installed for the next couple of weeks if you can. Google asks testers to stay opted in during the test period, and it really helps.
