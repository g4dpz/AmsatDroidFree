Subject: AmsatDroid Free resurrected - new Android satellite tracker

Hi all,

I'm pleased to say I've resurrected AmsatDroid Free as a completely new
implementation. The old app had aged out with the Android platform, so rather
than patch it I rebuilt it from the ground up in Kotlin with a modern Jetpack
Compose interface, while keeping the same proven SGP4/SDP4 orbit-prediction
core (predict4java) under a regression test so predictions stay faithful.

What it does:

- Pass prediction for your location, with a sky view and a ground-track map
- Doppler-corrected downlink and uplink frequencies, updated live during a
  pass, including inverting (U/V) transponders
- Transponder frequencies come from Peter Goodhall's (MM9SQL) OscarWatch
  database, synced automatically so they stay current
- Pass alerts with a countdown notification and optional spoken announcements
- Compass-oriented sky chart and an aiming aid for hand-held antennas
- An all-satellite overview of what's up now and coming soon
- A home-screen widget for your next pass
- Automatic Keplerian element updates (Celestrak or AMSAT)
- Tablet and foldable layouts, per-app language, and a red night mode for
  observing without spoiling your night vision

It runs on Android 11 and later and is free and open source (GPL-3.0). The
source, with screenshots, is on GitHub:

https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree

It will also be available on the Google Play Store in a few days, once account
setup completes - I'll follow up here with the link when it's live.

Feedback, bug reports and pull requests are all welcome.
