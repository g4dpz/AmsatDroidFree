AmsatDroid test script (version 2.0.3)
======================================

This takes about 20 minutes, plus one real satellite pass for part 5. You don't
have to do it all at once, or in this order. For each step, note:

  OK       it did what the step says
  PROBLEM  it didn't, or something looked wrong (say what you saw)
  SKIPPED  you couldn't try it

Then send me your results by replying to my email, in the AmsatDroid group, or
using the bug form: https://github.com/g4dpz/AmsatDroidFree/issues/new/choose

Please include your device, your Android version, and the app version (menu,
then About). Don't send your exact home coordinates; a four-character locator
such as IO91 is enough. A screenshot helps with anything that looks wrong.

The menu is the three dots at the top right of the main screen.


1. First start (new installs only)
----------------------------------

1.1  Open the app before setting your location. Choose a satellite and press
     Calculate pass. It should ask you to set your home location, and show no
     passes.

1.2  Was it clear what to do next? Tell me either way.


2. Home location
----------------

2.1  Menu, Set home location. Type your locator (for example IO91wm). The
     latitude and longitude should fill in to match.

2.2  Type a latitude and longitude instead. The locator should update.

2.3  Press Use GPS, then Use network location. Each should fill in your
     position (GPS can take a minute outdoors). Allow the permission when
     asked.

2.4  Optional: deny the location permission. The app should explain, not
     crash, and you should still be able to type a locator.


3. Keps (orbital data)
----------------------

3.1  Open the menu. Under the Update keps heading, tap
     "Amateur (celestrak.org)". It should report that the keps were updated,
     or say when they can be downloaded again.

3.2  Open the menu again and, under Update keps, tap
     "New launches (celestrak.org)". The satellite list should change to the
     new launches.

3.3  Now both sources are stored. In Settings, under Orbital elements (keps),
     change Keps source back to "Amateur (celestrak.org)", then to
     "New launches (celestrak.org)" again. The satellite list should change
     straight away each time, with no download or wait.

3.4  Finally, set Keps source back to "Amateur (celestrak.org)". New
     launches only lists satellites launched in the last 30 days, so the
     rest of this script needs Amateur.


4. Passes
---------

4.1  Choose a satellite you know, such as ISS or SO-50, set "Calculate passes
     for the next" to 24 hours, and press Calculate pass.

4.2  Compare a few pass times with a tracker you trust (another app or a
     website). Note any that differ by more than a minute.

4.3  Press Change start time, pick tomorrow, and calculate again. Then press
     Use current time and press Calculate pass once more, so the list shows
     passes from now again.

4.4  Rotate the device. The list and your choices should stay.


5. Sky view, Doppler and Aim (best during a real pass)
------------------------------------------------------

5.1  Tap a pass. The sky chart should show the track from AOS to LOS.

5.2  Turn Compass on. The chart should turn as you turn the device. If it
     asks, move the device in a figure of eight to calibrate.

5.3  During a pass, check the Frequencies (Doppler) card below the chart.
     If you can, tune to the "Tune" frequency and say whether you hear the
     satellite.

5.4  Turn Aim on and follow the arrows. Does "On target" match where the
     satellite really is? Keep the phone away from your radio while doing
     this, because metal disturbs the compass.


6. Alerts
---------

6.1  Long-press a pass in the next hour or two to set an alert. Allow
     notifications when asked.

6.2  Did the alert arrive at the time set in Settings, Alert lead time?
     Note how early or late it was.

6.3  Optional: turn on Speak pass alerts in Settings and check you hear it.


7. Other screens
----------------

7.1  Menu, Map view. You should see the ground track, the footprint and your
     home position. If the main screen already shows the map (on some large
     tablets held sideways), Map view isn't in the menu; check that map
     instead.

7.2  Menu, All satellites. "Up now" and the coming passes should fill in
     after a few seconds.

7.3  Add the AmsatDroid widget to your home screen. It should show the next
     pass of your selected satellite.

7.4  In Settings, set Theme to Red night. Everything should be readable in red.


8. Anything else
----------------

8.1  Did anything look cramped, cut off or hard to read on your device?

8.2  Did the app close unexpectedly at any point? If so, what were you doing?

8.3  What did you like, and what would you change?


Thank you for testing.
