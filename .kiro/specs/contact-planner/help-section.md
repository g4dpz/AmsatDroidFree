## Contact planner
Menu, **Contact planner**, finds when two stations can both use a satellite. It is for planning only: it has no alerts and no Doppler figures. Use the sky screen or your tracking software at the time of the contact.

1. Under **Stations**, choose **Add station** and enter a name and a locator (4, 6 or 8 characters) or latitude and longitude. You can save up to 10 stations. Your own station is your home location.
2. Choose the other station, and the **Minimum elevation** that both stations must reach (0 to 30 degrees).
3. Choose the **Time window** (12, 24 or 48 hours) and whether to search the selected satellite or all satellites.
4. The **Mutual windows** list shows each window with its start, end, length and best moment. A window of less than a minute may be missed.
5. Tap a window to see the total path at the best moment, then each station's azimuth, elevation and range, and a timeline through the window. Tap it again to hide the detail.

The best moment is when the lower of the two elevations is highest.
